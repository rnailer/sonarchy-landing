/ how it works /

Press play.

01
/ gather /

One phone hosts.
Everyone joins.

The host runs the music through a speaker. Everyone else joins on their own phone with a four-digit code. 2–8 players, no installs, no logins.

02
/ pick /

The category
drops.

"Songs that sound like summer." "Songs your ex ruined." "Songs that go hard at 2am." Search YouTube on your phone and lock in the track that nails it.

03
/ guess /

The songs play.
Name them.

Each pick plays through the speaker. Be first to name the song — add the artist for extra points. Then the reveal drops: the track, the score, and whose pick it really was.

04
/ rate /

Rate it.
Out loud.

Score every song 1–10 on how well it fit — names attached, no hiding. Two cassette mascots narrate the fallout. Highest score across the rounds takes the crown.
